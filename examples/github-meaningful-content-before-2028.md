## 【卦象解读 · Interpretation】

### Structure first

Standstill is traditionally read as a lack of exchange between Heaven above and Earth below. As a reflective image, it suggests potential whose connection to receptive ground is obstructed.

Enthusiasm introduces Thunder above the same Earth: movement meets a receptive foundation. The symbolic transition suggests examining how blocked energy could become organized activity and participation.

### Application to the question

My interpretation is cautiously encouraging about meaningful impact before 2028, provided ideas become a coherent body of work that someone else can enter and use. A possible obstacle to examine is valuable material whose purpose or practical application is difficult for readers to grasp. The cast does not establish that this is true of the user's repositories.

In this reading, success means people finding the work useful, learning from it, or building on it. The reading supplies no reliable estimate of stars, followers, probabilities, or the timing of recognition.

## 【动爻重点 · Changing lines】

The following traditional themes are paraphrases informed by the [received line passages for hexagram 12](https://yiatlas.com/en/hexagrams/pi). The GitHub applications are modern interpretation, not ancient text.

- **Line 5 — secure the improvement.** Its traditional theme is ending obstruction while remaining attentive to fragility. Applied to GitHub, protect progress with clear explanations, working examples, and steady maintenance.
- **Line 6 — overturn the blockage.** Its traditional movement is from obstruction toward joy. Applied to GitHub, reconsider a presentation or publishing approach that repeatedly fails to connect with readers.

## 【行动／反思 · Practical prompts】

1. Choose one contribution you want people to remember your GitHub for by the end of 2027.
2. Give its main repository a clear purpose, a usable example, and an obvious first step.
3. Look for evidence of meaning: someone reusing the work, learning from it, or offering substantive feedback.
